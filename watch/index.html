What the montage shows

ROBINSPECT is a vendor-agnostic robotic NDT platform: a 6-axis robot, advanced ultrasonics and a repeatable path on complex geometry. What the video shows is contact UT on the filmed cell. PAUT / TFM are part of the integration stack and PoC scope — not proven by this footage.

  1. 0:00 – 0:30 · 3D scan. Point cloud of the part, then the mesh used for path planning.
  2. 0:30 – 1:22 · Trajectory. Tool path generated on the surface, normal to the weld.
  3. 1:23 – 5:04 · Robot setup. Cell, coupling, probe on the part — the slow, real preparation.
  4. 5:04 – 6:03 · Path quality. Check that the TCP (tool center point) follows the weld before acquisition.
  5. 6:03 – 7:12 · Contact UT. Synchronized contact UT recording along the trajectory. PAUT and TFM: can be integrated, still to validate in a PoC, not shown as proven here.

Times are those of the part in the video. A full cycle on the filmed part is about 30 minutes — measured on that part only, not a universal SLA. Acquisition itself is under a minute; the 3D scan about 30 seconds; trajectory in a few seconds. UT expertise stays critical behind the cell.
