Field computation

UT and phased array beam field computation

This is what simUT computes today: the emitted beam field, on a desktop workflow an NDT engineer can set up and read.

Phased array focusing (UTPA)

The same 32-element linear array, focused with UTPA and shown at the same scale, without and with beam steering.

Inputs and outputs

What you set, and what you read.

Inputs

  • Single-element probes
  • Phased array probes
  • Contact coupling, on flat parts
  • Immersion coupling, on flat parts
  • Wedge coupling, on flat parts

Curved geometries Coming

Material, probe parameters, and a computation zone (a planar cut or a volume on a regular grid) complete the setup.

Outputs

  • A 2D field map in the computation plane
  • A profile along the beam axis
  • A 3D view of the probe, the part, and the computation zone
  • PNG image and CSV data export from the results view

The field computation runs multithreaded on the CPU, with GPU acceleration.
