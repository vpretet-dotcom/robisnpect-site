Why we built simUT

Simulation you can actually use

simUT was started by an NDT engineer who'd spent years in the nuclear industry, then in phased array UT, imaging and simulation. Again and again he ran into the same wall: simulation tools that were slow to optimize, hard to use, and out of reach for anyone without specialist training. So he built a new engine, designed for parallel computing on CPU and GPU and easy to pick up, to help with inspection preparation, project work and making sense of what you see in the data.
