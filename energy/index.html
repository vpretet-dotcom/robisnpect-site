Use case · Constrained environments

Inspection where
people should not linger.

We integrate robotic ultrasonic inspection for constrained sites — your robot, your UT/PAUT/TFM: less time in the volume, a replayable path. Today: filmed lab demo (contact UT). PAUT and TFM: can be integrated, still to validate in a PoC. Next: PoCs.

Milieu contraignant, in engineering terms

Many of the welds and assets that matter sit in nuclear islands, energy piping, tanks and cells where time-on-task is limited. Manual NDT there is possible — it is expensive, slow to document, and hard to repeat after an outage.

ROBINSPECT started on this kind of site (NDT Level 2 UT and eddy current, nuclear and aerospace). The product is not “a robot in a cage”. It is inspection engineering for constrained sites: access, coupling, trajectory, then a replayable UT / PAUT / TFM scan.

What changes for the site

  • Less human time in the constrained volume.
  • The same path next outage — traceable, auditable.
  • No lock-in to one robot OEM or one ultrasonic brand.

Once the cell is taught, a full cycle is about 30 minutes, measured on the filmed coupon only — not a universal SLA. NDT Level 2 stays behind the cell for acquisition and interpretation. Acquisition itself is short; preparation is the real work, and we design that too.
