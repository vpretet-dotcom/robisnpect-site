Use case · Constrained environments

Inspection where
people should not linger.

Confined, radiological, hot, or simply hard to access: the cost is HSE, not the coupon. A vendor-agnostic 6-axis cell puts the probe in the constraint and keeps the inspector in control of the data.

Milieu contraignant, in engineering terms

Many of the welds and assets that matter sit in nuclear islands, energy piping, tanks and cells where time-on-task is limited. Manual NDT there is possible — it is expensive, slow to document, and hard to repeat after an outage.

ROBINSPECT comes from that shop floor (NDT Level 2 UT & ET, nuclear and aerospace). The product is not “a robot in a cage”. It is inspection engineering for constrained sites: access, coupling, trajectory, then a replayable UT / PAUT / TFM scan.

What changes for the site

  • Less human time in the constrained volume.
  • The same path next outage — traceable, auditable.
  • No lock-in to one robot OEM or one ultrasonic brand.

A full cycle on a typical part is on the order of 30 minutes once the cell is taught, without a roboticist standing there. Acquisition itself is short; preparation is the real work, and we design that too.
