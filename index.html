The challenge

Too manual.
Too variable.
Too hard to scale.

Why NDT automation is critical on complex geometries: the industry still depends on the gesture of the operator.

High operator dependency

Inspection quality still varies significantly between operators. Complex geometries make consistent probe orientation and coupling extremely difficult to repeat.

Cost, time & scalability

Long inspection cycles, production downtime, and a growing shortage of qualified Level 2/3 experts create bottlenecks for critical assets.

Traceability & audit burden

Manual documentation is slow, error-prone and often disconnected from the actual inspection data and robot path.

Safety & access constraints

Many high-value inspections occur in confined, radiological or hazardous environments where manual execution carries high HSE cost.

FAQ

Robotic NDT, in plain terms.

What is robotic NDT inspection?
Robotic NDT (non-destructive testing) uses a 6-axis robot to move an ultrasonic probe along a part — welds, curved skins, complex geometries — with a repeatable path, stable orientation and full traceability. ROBINSPECT builds that inspection system, not a generic robot cell.
Can a robot perform ultrasonic testing (UT, PAUT, TFM)?
Yes, if trajectory, coupling and acquisition are designed together. ROBINSPECT integrates UT, PAUT and TFM/ATFM with robot position so the scan is synchronized — the same idea as a skilled inspector, without the variability of the gesture.
Which industries need automated NDT robotics?
Aerospace and space structures, energy and nuclear assets, heavy industry and any site where critical components are expensive to stop, hard to access, or must be documented for audit.
Are you locked to one robot or sensor brand?
No. The platform is vendor-agnostic: multi-robot, multi-sensor. The value is the inspection workflow — geometry, trajectory, acquisition and report — not a single hardware stack.
Where is ROBINSPECT based?
Belgium, with a focus on Europe and the United States. Technical discussions are already open with NDT, robotics and high-value industrial players.
