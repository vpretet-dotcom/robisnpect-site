FAQ

Robotic NDT, in plain terms.

What is robotic NDT inspection?
Robotic NDT (non-destructive testing) uses a 6-axis robot to move an ultrasonic probe along a part — welds, curved skins, complex geometries — with a repeatable path, stable orientation and inspection-data traceability. ROBINSPECT builds that inspection system, not a generic robot cell.
Can a robot perform UT, phased array ultrasonic testing (PAUT) or total focusing method (TFM)?
Yes, if trajectory, coupling and acquisition are designed together. ROBINSPECT syncs contact UT with robot position, as shown on the filmed lab cell. PAUT and TFM are also demonstrated on real hardware. The gesture of a skilled inspector, without operator-to-operator variability.
Which industries need automated NDT robotics?
Aerospace and space structures, energy and nuclear assets, heavy industry and any site where critical components are expensive to stop, hard to access, or must be documented for audit.
Are you locked to one robot or sensor brand?
No. The platform is vendor-agnostic: multi-robot, multi-sensor. The value is the inspection workflow — geometry, trajectory, acquisition and report — not a single hardware stack.
Are you a robot manufacturer or an integrator?
Integrator. ROBINSPECT does inspection engineering: geometry, trajectory, coupling, UT/PAUT/TFM acquisition and the report. Vendor-agnostic — multi-robot, multi-sensor. We do not lock you into one OEM.
Do you work on constrained sites?
Yes. Confined, nuclear, sites with high HSE constraints are a core use case: less time in the constrained volume, a replayable path, Level 2 expertise behind the cell (UT and eddy current). Constrained sites.
Where is ROBINSPECT based?
In Orlando, Florida, USA, also active in Europe. Technical discussions are already open with NDT, robotics and high-value industrial players.
