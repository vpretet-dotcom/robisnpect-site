Use case · Welds

Robotic inspection
of welds.

We integrate robotic ultrasonic inspection for welds — your robot, your UT/PAUT/TFM: keep the probe at the surface normal, couple it, record on a path you can replay. Today: filmed lab demo (contact UT). PAUT and TFM: can be integrated, still to validate in a PoC. Next: PoCs.

What this page is about

Manual weld inspection still depends on the inspector’s technique. On long seams, nozzles and curved joints, probe orientation and coupling drift. Quality varies between shifts. The report is often disconnected from where the probe actually was.

ROBINSPECT is a robotic NDT integrator: we do not sell a robot. We design the inspection engineering — 3D geometry, trajectory on the weld, probe at the surface normal, coupling, and synchronized UT acquisition (PAUT and TFM in PoC).

The workflow

  • Scan the part (minutes, not a metrology lab).
  • Generate a tool path on the weld, in a few seconds.
  • Set up the cell once — then the path is replayable.
  • Acquire UT, PAUT or TFM along that path, typically under a minute on the filmed coupon.

Once the cell is taught, a full cycle is about 30 minutes, measured on the filmed coupon only — not a universal SLA. A Level 2 inspector still signs off acquisition and interpretation.

Vendor-agnostic on purpose

The value is the workflow, not a locked hardware stack. Multi-robot, multi-sensor. If you already have a PAUT instrument or a 6-axis arm, we integrate around it.
